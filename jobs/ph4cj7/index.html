Sales Associate | Luxury Fragrance & Beauty (Covent Garden and King's Road)

London, UK

Job description

About Granado
Founded in Rio de Janeiro in 1870, Granado is an iconic Brazilian beauty and fragrance brand known for its products inspired by tropical plants, herbs and flowers. With over 150 years of history, the brand has expanded internationally, with stores across Europe and a presence in some of the most prestigious department stores.

The Role

We are looking for Sales Associates to join our stores on King's Road and Covent Garden, London, on a full-time, permanent basis.

You will deliver exceptional customer experiences, share Granado's history and product expertise, drive sales and build lasting customer relationships while representing the brand's values.

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide outstanding, personalised customer service and product recommendations.
  • Share the brand's story and communicate product features with enthusiasm.
  • Build customer loyalty and contribute to sales targets.
  • Maintain excellent store presentation, visual merchandising and product displays.
  • Support stock replenishment, inventory management and product exchanges.
  • Help ensure a clean, organised and welcoming store environment.

About You

  • 2–3 years of retail sales experience; beauty, cosmetics or fragrance experience is a plus.
  • Passion for sales, customer service and building customer relationships.
  • Strong communication skills and a results-driven mindset.
  • Fluent in English; Portuguese or other languages are an advantage.
  • Flexible to work according to business needs.
  • Eligible to live and work in the UK.

What We Offer

  • Competitive base salary plus sales commission.
  • Employee benefits package.
  • Company pension.
  • Employee discount.
  • Flexible working arrangements.
  • Diversity & Inclusion

Granado is an equal opportunity employer and welcomes applicants from all backgrounds.

Interested in Joining Us?

If you are passionate about beauty, fragrance and delivering exceptional customer experiences, we would love to hear from you. Send us your CV and become part of the Granado journey!
